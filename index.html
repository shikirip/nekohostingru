      機能 Features
      
Infrastructure that purrs.

      
The boring parts of hosting, done properly — so you can get back to building things.

    

    

      

        
命

        
Nine Lives™ failover

        
Every instance is backed by eight warm standbys. When one goes down, another one wakes up. We have never needed the ninth.

      

      

        
網

        
Global edge network

        
312 points of presence on six continents. Your content is cached close to your users and simply sits there, comfortably, for as long as you need it.

      

      

        
眠

        
Managed Postgres

        
Automatic backups every 24 hours. Internally we call them naps. Restore from any nap in the last 30 days with one command.

      

      

        
盾

        
DDoS protection

        
Always on, at no extra cost. Suspicious traffic is identified, isolated and hissed at before it reaches your origin.

      

      

        
速

        
Zero-downtime deploys

        
Rolling deploys with instant rollback. Deploys are paused daily between 14:00 and 16:00 JST. This is not negotiable.

      

      

        
話

        
Support that shows up

        
Real engineers answer every ticket, around the clock. Median first response is four minutes, or one sincere slow blink.

      質問 FAQ
      
    Questions, answered.
    
    
    
    
    
      
    
        Is nekohosting a real company?
        
    We get asked this surprisingly often. We are registered, we have servers, and the servers have names. Make of that what you will.
    
      
    
      
    
        What exactly is the nap window?
        
    Between 14:00 and 16:00 JST, new deploys are queued instead of executed. Everything already running keeps running. The infrastructure team rests. Incident rates have dropped 30% since we introduced it, and we no longer question it.
    
      
    
      
    
        What happens when an instance uses all nine lives?
        
    It hasn't happened yet. There is a runbook. It is one page long and mostly a drawing.
    
      
    
      
    
        Where are your data centers?
        
    Tokyo, Osaka, Singapore, Frankfurt, Helsinki, Oregon and São Paulo, all in Tier III facilities. The Ohio region runs out of a large cardboard box, at its own request.
    
      
    
      
    
        Do you support Docker?
        
    Yes. Containers of any size are welcome. Honestly, any box.
    
      
    
      
    
        Can I get a refund?
        
    Yes — within 30 days, no questions asked. Please don't ask the server directly. It takes it personally.
